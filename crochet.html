✦ About Me

Hey, it's Nick!

I've been crocheting since I was a kid, and I never stopped. There's something magical about turning a single strand of yarn into something soft, squishy, and full of personality — whether it's an amigurumi frog with tiny legs or a cosy cardigan that feels like a hug.

Every item in this shop is handmade by me, with care and a silly amount of love. Custom orders welcome — if you can dream it, I can (probably) crochet it!
