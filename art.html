✦ About Me

Hello, I'm Nick!

I'm a digital artist with a passion for soft, dreamy illustrations that tell stories through colour and light. My art lives in the space between fantasy and emotion — characters with big feelings, florals that breathe, and worlds you'd want to step into.

Every piece I create is made with love, care, and way too many reference tabs open. Whether it's a portrait, a scene, or something entirely surreal — I pour my whole heart into it.
